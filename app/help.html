Frequently Asked Questions

How does the delivery code work?
When your driver arrives, you will receive a 4-digit code in your app Inbox. You must enter this code on the Verification page to officially mark the order as Delivered and secure your package.
Where can I track my order?
You can track all active and past orders by going to your Profile and clicking on the "Orders" tab. The status badge will change color as your order progresses!
What is your return policy?
Because we deal with fresh produce, we offer a 24-hour freshness guarantee. If any item is damaged or not fresh, please contact support immediately for a replacement or refund.
